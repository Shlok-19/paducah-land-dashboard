Study limitations
  • Bridge clearance changes with river stage and must be refreshed for the voyage date.
  • Actual controlling depth, shoaling and dredging conditions must be checked before movement.
  • Movable bridge status, lock outages and temporary restrictions require current confirmation.
  • The exact Burlington unloading location can change the governing air-draft requirement.
  • Tow configuration, horsepower, current, wind and maneuverability require marine-operator review.
  • Final cargo loading requires actual barge hydrostatics, stability, ballast, freeboard, deck/point-load and grillage checks.
