Study limitations
  • This is a preliminary engineering and logistics screening tool, not navigation approval.
  • Bridge clearances and controlling depths are stage- and date-dependent; refresh them before movement.
  • Required draft is an approximate rectangular-hull freshwater calculation until actual barge hydrostatic tables are used.
  • Average deck pressure does not verify concentrated reactions, grillage, local deck strength or longitudinal strength.
  • Final loading requires actual stability, GM, trim, heel, ballast, freeboard/load-line and securing calculations.
  • Tow configuration, horsepower, current, wind, pilotage, berth geometry and maneuverability require marine-operator review.
  • The exact Burlington unloading point determines which destination bridges are actually on the final route.
