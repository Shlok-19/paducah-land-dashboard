Study Limitations

  • Bridge clearance changes with river stage.
  • Actual controlling depth and shoaling must be checked for the voyage date.
  • Movable bridge status and lock outages/restrictions require current confirmation.
  • Exact Burlington unloading location can change the governing air-draft limit.
  • Tow configuration, horsepower, current, wind and maneuverability require marine-operator review.
  • Cargo center of gravity, deck/point loading, ballast, stability and freeboard require barge-specific engineering.

Key Terms

RM
River Mile; identifies a location along the river.
Air Draft
Water surface to highest point of barge/cargo.
Loaded Draft
Depth the loaded barge extends below the water surface.
Beam
Maximum barge width.
Bridge Clearance
Vertical space between water and the controlling bridge structure.
Navigation Opening
Usable horizontal bridge opening for vessels.
Lock Chamber
Enclosed lock area used to raise/lower vessels.
One-Cut Lockage
Barge and towboat pass the lock together.
Displacement
Total vessel/cargo weight supported by displaced water.
Lightship
Empty barge weight before cargo.
Freeboard
Waterline-to-deck-edge vertical distance.
PASS / CHECK / FAIL
Within screen / needs verification / exceeds screen.
